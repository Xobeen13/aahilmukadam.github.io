About Me

I’m Aahil Mukadam, a mechatronics engineering student interested in how mechanical systems, controls, software, and data interact within larger engineering systems.

My experience has included HVAC and retrofit work in buildings, plant-wide energy and manufacturing systems at Toyota, CAD, embedded software, robotics, and hands-on automotive work. These experiences have shaped my interest in systems integration and practical engineering design.

I’m also exploring biomedical engineering and data-driven analysis, including sports and performance analytics across basketball, football, and competitive gaming. Across these areas, I’m most interested in understanding complex systems and finding practical ways to improve them.
